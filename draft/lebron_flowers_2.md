# I'm a MJ Guy. LeBron Deserves His Flowers.

Let me get this out of the way up front: Michael Jordan is the greatest basketball player who ever lived. My first basketball memory is MJ hitting that jumper over Georgetown to win the national championship for North Carolina in 1982. I was hooked. Been a Jordan guy ever since.

So when I tell you that what LeBron James is doing right now is genuinely insane, I need you to understand that's not a hot take from a LeBron stan. That's a confession from someone who has spent forty-plus years arguing the other side.

The man is 41 years old. He's averaging 25.3 points, 9.7 rebounds, and 8.7 assists in the 2026 playoffs. The Lakers are up 3-0 on Houston, one game from the second round. And he's doing it without Luka Dončić — the reigning scoring champion and the guy this team is actually built around — who has been sidelined since April 2 with a Grade 2 hamstring strain and isn't expected back until at least the second round. Austin Reaves is out too.

LeBron is not carrying a championship roster. He's carrying a roster.

---

## The Thought Experiment That Breaks Your Brain

Here's what I keep coming back to. Pull out a star or two from every legitimate contender and ask yourself honestly whether they're still winning their first-round series.

Take Shai Gilgeous-Alexander and Chet Holmgren off Oklahoma City. The best team in the West, built around two of the ten best players in the league. Without them, the Thunder don't make the postseason conversation, let alone the first round.

Take Victor Wembanyama and De'Aaron Fox off San Antonio. The Spurs won 60-plus games this season — a legitimate contender. Without those two, they're a lottery team with good energy.

Take Jayson Tatum and Jaylen Brown off Boston. The Celtics become a nice collection of role players who are watching the second round from their couches.

And here's the one that really lands: take Nikola Jokic and Jamal Murray off Denver. The two-time Finals team. The guy many consider the most skilled offensive player alive. Without Jokic, the Nuggets aren't a first-round threat. And with him? They're currently *losing* to Minnesota in their first-round series.

Now take LeBron off the Lakers.

Without him — no Luka, no Reaves, no LeBron — this roster doesn't win a playoff game. With him, they're three wins from the second round.

---

## A List of Exactly One

I want to give you some historical context on 41-year-olds averaging 25 points per game in the NBA playoffs.

That list has one name on it.

The previous oldest player to even *score* in a playoff game was Robert Parish, who dropped 2 points for the Chicago Bulls at age 43. Not 2 points per game — 2 points total, as a ceremonial cap piece at the end of a career. That's the pre-LeBron data point on NBA elders in the postseason.

LeBron is averaging 25.3 points, 9.7 rebounds, and 8.7 assists through three games. His Game 3 line — 29 points, 13 rebounds, 6 assists, 3 steals, and a block in 45 minutes of overtime — would be a career highlight for most players in the league right now. For LeBron at 41, it was a Tuesday.

There is no historical comparison because history didn't think to make room for this.

---

## The Flowers

I'm not here to relitigate the GOAT debate. MJ is still the GOAT to me and I don't think that changes. Six rings, six Finals MVPs, never lost in the Finals — that's a case that doesn't break.

But "who's the greatest" and "give this man his credit" are two different conversations, and we've spent so much energy on the first one that we've been stingy with the second.

LeBron James is doing something in 2026 that nobody has ever done, at an age when most players are either retired, doing analyst work on ESPN, or logging 8 ceremonial minutes a night. He is *the guy* on a playoff team, winning games in April, and right now he's doing it with his two best teammates watching from the sideline in street clothes.

Michael Jordan was the greatest. But nobody in the history of professional basketball has done what LeBron James is doing right now.

That deserves some flowers.
