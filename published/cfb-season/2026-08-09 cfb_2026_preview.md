# That's My Teammate. That's My Quarterback

Is it me, or is everyone bringing back a potential 1st round quarterback this year?  Ohio State returns Julian Sayin. Notre Dame returns CJ Carr. Texas returns Arch Manning. Oregon returns Dante Moore.  Oklahoma returns John Mateer.  Miami couldn't bring back the Tommy Boy of College Football in Carson Beck, so the U had to buy a quarterback on the open market.  

So, when everyboy has an elite signal caller, who has the edge? The real question becomes about what's standing next to the quarterback, and did any side of the ball get hollowed out last April.

## Just Sayin', Jeremiah is still REALLY Good

Ohio State is the preseason No. 1 and it will be led by the offense. Sayin completed 77% of his throws for 3,610 yards and 32 touchdowns, the third-best single-season completion rate in FBS history. Jeremiah Smith is a good month from owning every receiving record the program has. Bo Jackson ran for 1,090 yards as a freshman and the backfield also gained a Legend. Four of five linemen return, and Ryan Day imported Arthur Smith from the Steelers to make it all work.

So, what's the problem? Seven Buckeye defenders got drafted, including Arvell Reese fifth, Sonny Styles seventh and Caleb Downs eleventh. That unit finished No. 1 in the country. Matt Patricia is the highest-paid assistant in the sport at a guaranteed $3.75 million, and he did this exact rebuild last year using blue-chippers already sitting in the room. This year he's tyring to do it with transfers.

Downs might go down as the single biggest loss for any team this year compared to last.  He was the ultimate eraser on the back end cleaning up mistakes and making up for other players being out of position with an instinct that won't be replaced by any single player.

A positive note for the Buckeyes: In 2024, Indiana's only two losses were to Notre Dame and Ohio State.  The two teams who played for the National Championship that year.  In 2025, Ohio State's only two losses were to Indiana and Miami.  Yep, you're catching on, they were the two teams that played for the championship last year.  That's the résumé of a team that's about to win it all. 

## Is Miami Mensah Smart?

The Hurricanes lost the title game to Indiana 27-21 after knocking off Ohio State and Ole Miss to get there. Then they lost Carson Beck, Francis Mauigoa and Rueben Bain Jr., and reloaded in about nine days.

Darian Mensah arrives from Duke with 3,973 yards, 34 touchdowns and six interceptions, and the distinction of having been *sued by his own university* to stop him from leaving. Duke settled. Mensah brought his top receiver, Cooper Barkate. Damon Wilson II comes from Missouri with nine sacks to replace Bain. Mark Fletcher Jr. is back after 1,192 yards.

But the whole thing orbits Malachi Toney, who caught 109 passes for 1,211 yards as a true freshman who spent most of the season being seventeen. He led all of FBS in receptions, and he's the answer to "best receiver in college football" if you're willing to ignore number 4.

Circle November 7 in South Bend. Miami beat Notre Dame 27-24 in Week 1 last season, and that one result is the entire reason Miami played for a title and Notre Dame took their ball and went home for the holidays.

## Not Lucky in Love?

Carr threw for 2,741 yards with 24 touchdowns and six picks as a redshirt freshman and is now a real Heisman candidate. Jordan Faison is back, Leonard Moore might be the best cover corner in the sport, and 14 of the 21 defenders who played 200-plus snaps return.

What's gone is the safety valve. Jeremiyah Love went third overall, the highest drafted running back since Saquon Barkley, and Aneyas Williams inherits the job with roughly nothing on his résumé. Carr has never had to be the entire offense. Now he is.

The schedule is the real story: only three ranked opponents, and 10-2 already got them left out once. A September 6 opener at Lambeau against Wisconsin could be a sneaky tough test, but the luck of the Irish still survives since the Badgers decided to retain Luck Fickell and they probably still don't have a quarterback.

## We're Baaaacckkk, We think.

Texas opened last year as preseason No. 1 and finished 9-3 and out of the playoffs, despite some very solid complaining from Sark. The autopsy isn't complicated. The line entered 2025 with one returning starter, Texas averaged 3.3 yards a carry in SEC play, and Manning spent September running for his life. He completed 44% against UTEP. That's the version everyone remembers.

The version that matters came after. Over his final eight games Manning threw for 2,012 yards with 15 touchdowns and two interceptions. Same player, functioning line.

Three linemen return now, headlined by likely first-rounder Trevor Goosby, with Melvin Siani and Laurence Seymore imported to fill the rest. And Colin Simmons already has 21 sacks and 29.5 tackles for loss in two seasons, which is why the sharp play at your Labor Day cookout is arguing he's the first Longhorn off the board in 2027, not the guy with the famous last name.

Ohio State comes to Austin September 12. If they win, we get to hear how Texas is really back, and for real this time. If they lose, get ready for the politicking to secure a spot in the playoffs.

## Dante's Inferno

Every Oregon story this offseason is a decision not made. Dante Moore was in the No. 1 overall draft pick conversation and came back. So did Matayo Uiagalelei, Teitum Tuioti and center Iapani Laloulu. The entire starting defensive front returns because none of them filed the paperwork.

Jordon Davison and Dierre Hill Jr. combined for 1,323 rushing yards as freshman backups and are now the feature guys. Evan Stewart returns from the patellar injury that erased his 2025. Kenyon Sadiq is off to the NFL, but Jamari Johnson is more than capable of stepping into the lead TE role.

The risk is invisible on the depth chart. Oregon lost both coordinators to head coaching jobs, Will Stein to Kentucky and Tosh Lupoi to Cal. Continuity in the huddle is not continuity in the headset, and the last time we saw this roster it lost 56-22 to Indiana with a season on the line.

## Is Oklahoma OK?

Every darkhorse pitch requires a leap of faith. Oklahoma's is the shortest in the country.

The Sooners had an elite defense last year, fourth in SP+, third against the run, 15.5 points allowed a game.  The defense dragged the Sooners into the playoffs. The offense finished 51st in SP+ and 92nd in total offense. That's a canyon between two units wearing the same jersey.

Mateer is why. He broke a bone in his throwing thumb in the SEC opener, had surgery, and came back to face Texas seventeen days later with a rebuilt grip. Oklahoma never gained 400 yards in a game again. He finished with 14 touchdown passes against 11 interceptions after leading the nation in total touchdowns the year before at Washington State.

Here's the reminder worth sitting with: Joe Burrow's stat line the year before he had the greatest season college football has ever seen was 2,894 yards and 16 touchdowns, a line so unremarkable that scouts had him pegged as a fringe NFL prospect. One healthy offseason later he threw for 5,671 yards and 60 touchdowns and won the Heisman by the widest margin in the award's history. Mateer's most recent healthy season led the nation in total touchdowns, but what you remember from 2025 is a guy throwing with a rebuilt grip.

Now he's healthy, he has a year in Ben Arbuckle's system, four linemen return, and Kip Lewis, Taylor Wein, David Stone and both Bowen brothers hold the defense together. The bet isn't that Venables builds something new. It's that a working thumb is worth three or four wins.

## What I'm Actually Watching

Two of these six will probably look like frauds by Halloween, and I'm not going to pretend I know which two.

The tells are specific and they arrive early. Can Patricia teach a transfer-heavy defense to be Ohio State's defense before Austin on September 12? Can Manning behind a real line be more like the second-half Manning or the first-half one?  Can the Ducks' scheme survive losing both of the people who wrote it?

Since everyone seems to have a quarterback, we now have to see who has the better 21.
